Privacy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This policy explains what information AROHA ("we", "us") collects when you use our website, and how we use it. We only collect what we need to take and deliver your orders.

What we collect

  • Google sign-in details: when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from your Google account. We never see or store your Google password.
  • Order details: the name, mobile number, delivery address, delivery area, payment method, bKash transaction ID (if you pay by bKash) and any note you enter at checkout, plus the items you order.
  • Your bag: items you add to your bag are saved only in your own browser on your device.

We do not collect card numbers or bank details.

How we use it

  • To process, confirm and deliver your orders, and to call or email you about them.
  • To show you your order history on the "My orders" page.
  • To handle exchanges, returns and customer support.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase — stores our website's data and handles secure sign-in.
  • Google — provides the "Sign in with Google" service.
  • EmailJS — sends us an email notification when you place an order.
  • Delivery couriers — receive your name, phone number and address so they can deliver your parcel.

We only share what each service needs to do its job.

Your choices

  • You can browse the website without signing in. Signing in is only needed to place an order.
  • You can sign out at any time from the Account page.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information and order history by contacting us using the details below.
