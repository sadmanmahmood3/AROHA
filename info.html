Shipping

We deliver all over Bangladesh through trusted courier partners.

Payment

Cash on delivery

Pay the delivery rider in cash when your parcel arrives.

bKash

Send the total amount to our bKash number 01787456456 using Send Money, then enter the transaction ID at checkout. We confirm your order once the payment is checked.

Returns & exchange

  • Exchange within 7 days of delivery if the size doesn't fit or the item is faulty.
  • Items must be unworn, unwashed and have their tags attached.
  • Please check your parcel in front of the rider. If something is wrong, tell us straight away.
  • To start an exchange, contact us with your order number.
