Shipping

We deliver all over Bangladesh through trusted courier partners.

  • Inside Dhaka: ৳70 — usually 1–3 working days.
  • Outside Dhaka: ৳130 — usually 3–5 working days.
  • We call you to confirm every order before sending it.

Payment

Cash on delivery

Pay the delivery rider in cash when your parcel arrives. We call you to confirm every order before sending it.

Returns & exchange

  • We exchange an item within 7 days of delivery only if it is faulty — for example a defect, damage, or the wrong item was sent.
  • We don't exchange for size or change of mind, so please check the size guide before ordering.
  • The item must be unworn, unwashed and have its tags attached.
  • Please check your parcel in front of the rider. If something is wrong, tell us straight away.
  • To request an exchange, contact us with your order number and a photo of the fault.
